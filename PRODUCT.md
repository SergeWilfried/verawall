# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bank fraud-operations analysts (analysts, senior analysts, admins) working
desktop-first in the BIP Console: monitoring the alert queue, investigating
sessions, disposing held payments, managing cases and tenant settings. They
are in a high-stakes triage workflow — speed, density and trust matter more
than spectacle. A secondary marketing site (`/`) addresses bank risk
executives evaluating the platform; the console is the primary surface.

## Product Purpose

VeraWall is a behavioral intelligence platform for fraud prevention. The
console turns behavioral signals (typing, touch, device, network, ledger
flows) into actionable alerts and gives analysts the controls to contain
fraud in real time (hold/release payments, terminate sessions, escalate
cases). Success: an analyst can go from alert to confident decision in
under a minute, and trusts every number on screen.

## Brand Personality

Vigilant, precise, institutional. The shield-and-check mark and the
signature red (#D71A28) carry authority; everything else stays quiet,
dense and legible. Serious without being somber — the dark sidebar frames
a bright, calm working surface.

## Anti-references

- Consumer-fintech playfulness (rounded pastel cards, mascots, confetti).
- Generic SaaS dashboard chrome (gradient heroes, glassmorphism, oversized
  empty whitespace).
- Alarmism: red is for risk and primary actions only, never decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Density earns trust** — analysts scan tables and timelines; compact
   rows, strong alignment, tabular numbers.
2. **Red means risk or act** — the accent is reserved for scores, threats
   and primary actions; neutrals do the layout work.
3. **Same vocabulary everywhere** — Barlow for headings/labels/buttons,
   Open Sans for body, radius 3 controls on radius 6 white cards,
   1px #E3E7EB borders. New screens must be indistinguishable in idiom
   from existing ones.
4. **State is explicit** — every risk, session, case and integration shows
   its state as a colored chip or dot; never rely on position alone.
5. **No ceremony** — the console loads into work; transitions are
   150–250ms state feedback, never choreography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Desktop-first (analyst workstations); keyboard reachability for all
actions, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast on body text, reduced-motion
respected (motion is already minimal). Color-blind safety: state chips
always pair color with a text label.
