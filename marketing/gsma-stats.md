# GSMA stat pack — for the whitepaper / artifact

**Single source for all figures below:**
GSMA, *Mobile money fraud typologies and mitigation strategies*, March 2024.
Survey of mobile-money professionals across **34 countries in Africa, Asia and
Latin America**; 76% of respondents in mid-to-senior management roles.
Cite as: *GSMA, 2024.* (Loss-ratio footnote references GSMA SOTIR 2022.)

Every number here is verified against the source pages noted. Use verbatim.

## The headline gap — Verawall's wedge

| Stat | Figure | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| Providers using **AI / machine learning** in fraud management | **only 10%** (88% do *not*, 2% unsure) | The core wedge: the market runs on rules, not learned behavior. |
| Fraud **detected via customer complaint** | **96%** | Detection is reactive — the customer notices before the system does. |
| Providers who find their fraud system **"not so effective"** | **55%** (54.90%) | Systems exist but don't hold modern fraud. |
| Providers with **no formal fraud risk assessment** | **49%** (49.02%) | Half are blind to their own exposure. |
| Fraud cases with **low / very-low recovery** once money moves | **78%** (54% very-low 0–20% + 24% low 21–40%) | Prevention beats recovery — recovery barely happens. |

> GSMA's own words: *"The data may reflect a reactive stance towards fraud
> detection, heavily reliant on reports after the fact."*

## Scale / framing

| Stat | Figure |
|---|---|
| Average estimated **annual fraud loss per provider** | **$1.06 million** (≈0.03% of avg transaction volume/provider) |
| Believe mobile-money fraud is **generally increasing** | **84%** (63% steadily + 22% rapidly) |
| Countries surveyed | **34** (Africa, Asia, Latin America) |
| Post-COVID surge in mobile/online transactions as a fraud driver | **88%** (88.24%) |

## Typology prevalence — "most prevalent fraud schemes"

| Scheme | Prevalence | Verawall coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Identity fraud | **90%** (90.38%) | New-account / device signals |
| Social-engineering schemes | **88%** (88.46%) | Active-call / coached-transfer detection |
| Insider fraud | **86%** (86.54%) | (ledger + access controls) |
| **SIM-swap fraud** | **79%** (78.85%) | **The flagship ATO story** — SIM telemetry + behavioral baseline |
| Cyber fraud | **60%** (59.62%) | Device integrity, RAT, headless |
| **Agent commission (arbitrage) fraud** | **56%** (55.77%) | **Dedicated ledger typology nobody else covers** |
